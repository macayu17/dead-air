02 / BEHIND THE STATIC

A small model.
An overactive imagination.

Dead Air makes short, fictional ghost transmissions with SmolLM2, an open-weight language model running on your computer through Transformers.js. The dial sets the scene; your note gives it something to haunt.

The first run needs an internet connection to download the model from Hugging Face. After that, the cached model can work offline. Your notes and transmissions stay in this tab and the local server’s memory, unless you save a copy.

Small models lose the thread sometimes. Output may be odd, repetitive, or unfinished. All transmissions are invented; this receiver cannot contact people, living or dead.
